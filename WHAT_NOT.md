# Release-blocking design constraints

This file transcribes the supplied `WHAT NOT` document. Every item is a release-blocking review check.

1. Harsh ingredients
2. Lucide icons
3. Pure white background
4. Rainbow coloring
5. Drop shadows
6. Three feature cards in a row
7. Emojis
8. Liquid Glass
9. Em dashes
10. Inter, Geist or Space Grotesk
11. Coloured left stripe
12. Fake testimonials
13. Bento grids
14. Terminal window
15. "It is not x, it is y" style lines
16. Checkmark bullets
17. Three pricing tiers
18. No real product demos
19. Soft corner radius
20. Purple and black
21. Skeleton loaders
22. Radial orbs
23. Dot grids
24. Sparkle icons
25. Animated arrows
26. Terms of service page or generic TOS link
27. Privacy policy page or generic privacy-policy link
28. Generic hover animations
29. Neon colours
30. Basic pastel colours
31. Very small font that is difficult to read
32. Very large font that is aggressive or difficult to read
33. Fonts overlapping
34. API keys or secret keys anywhere in the code
35. The original item 35 contained no text. Do not invent an additional rule.

## Conservative interpretation

Where wording is ambiguous, choose the less generic and more readable interpretation. Do not use this ambiguity as permission to ignore a constraint.

The site must not collect first-party analytics or form data by default. This keeps the requested absence of generic policy pages honest. Contact can use direct email, WhatsApp and an external booking provider until a compliant first-party data flow is approved.
